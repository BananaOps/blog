## In short

This site is a static documentation site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics
and no tracking scripts, has no accounts and no forms. We collect nothing about
you.

## What this site does

Pages are pre-rendered files served as-is. There is no application server, no
database, and no third-party script loaded at page view.

The only data leaving your browser is what any web request requires: our host
receives your IP address, the requested URL, your user agent and a timestamp, in
its server logs. We do not have access to those logs as an identifiable dataset,
and we do not join them with anything else.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. GitHub processes the request data
described above as part of serving the site. Their handling of it is covered by
the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Fonts and external resources

Pages load their assets from the site itself. Where an external resource is used,
it is named in the page source.

## Our tools

This policy covers **this website only**. The tools we publish are self-hosted:
you run them on your own infrastructure, and the data they process never reaches
us. Each project's data handling is described in its own documentation.

## Links out

Our pages link to GitHub, Docker Hub and other third-party sites. Once you follow
such a link, that site's own policy applies.
